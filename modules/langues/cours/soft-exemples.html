What soft skills cover

“What are your soft skills?” In an interview or in the exam, you need to name some and recognise them in a situation.

A long list

Peggy Klaus, quoted in the course: “They cover a wide spectrum of abilities and traits: being self-aware, trustworthiness, conscientiousness, adaptability, critical thinking, attitude, initiative, empathy, confidence, integrity, self-control, organizational awareness, likability, influence, risk taking, problem solving, leadership, time management, and then some.”

The seven skills hiring managers look for in engineers

  1. Communication skills
  2. Interpersonal skills
  3. Problem solving skills
  4. Teamwork abilities
  5. Leadership and management skills
  6. Analytical skills
  7. Flexibility

Show a skill with one sentence

Added example: not from the course.

  • During my internship, the scanner software crashed before a test; I found the cause and restarted it safely. → problem solving
  • In our final-year project, I organised the tasks of our team of five. → leadership and management
  • When the project changed after two weeks, I adapted my part quickly. → flexibility
