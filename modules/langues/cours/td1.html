Interview questions

The sheet “Interview Questions – Practice” trains what chapter 2 announces: preparing your answers to typical questions. The sheet has no answer key, so the answers on this site are suggested answers. Exercises b, e and f come from a coursebook with audio (“Listen again”), and the audio was not sent.

Three types of questions

Every interview question asks for one of three things: talk about yourself (a), prove that you researched the company (b), show your skills and experience (c). Knowing the type tells you what to put in your answer.

Grammar for exercise e

  • after to, can, had to, able to, keen to, hope to: the base form (to motivate, can make);
  • after a preposition (about, in, before): the -ing form (worry about meeting);
  • after could be: the past participle, a passive (could be applied).
