Practice 1 : three types of questions

Write the type of question (a–c) next to each question (1–12). a : about yourself, your ambitions, your personality, your hobbies and interests. b : prove you have researched the job and company. c : demonstrate you have the skills and experience required.

  1. Can you tell us something about yourself?
  2. What experience do you have that is relevant for this position?
  3. What did you learn during your time at university?
  4. What do you see as your strengths?
  5. What do you think about our website?
  6. What attracted you to the position?
  7. How would your colleagues describe you?
  8. What sorts of projects did you work on in your last position?
  9. What university did you attend and why did you choose it?
  10. How do you typically approach new projects?
  11. What do you know about our company?
  12. Where do you see yourself in five years' time?
Voir la correction

Correction proposée : 1 a, 2 c, 3 c, 4 a, 5 b, 6 b, 7 a, 8 c, 9 a, 10 c, 11 b, 12 a.

Deux questions se discutent : 3 (ce qu'on a appris) se range en c si on répond par ses compétences, comme Alejandro ; 4 (les points forts) en a si on parle de sa personnalité, en c si on parle de compétences.
