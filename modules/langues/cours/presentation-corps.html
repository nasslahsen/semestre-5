Body language, visual aids, final tips

The audience watches as much as it listens: your body and your slides speak too.

Body language

  1. Smile
  2. Maintain eye contact
  3. Smooth and natural gestures
  4. Keep your hands out of your pockets
  5. Keep your shoulders straight

Visual aids

  • Keep them simple
  • Minimize
  • Use large fonts
  • Use colors
  • Avoid complete dependence on technology

Final tips

  • Prepare well and know your topic
  • Use humor
  • Don't lecture
  • Get enthusiastic
  • Keep time for questions
  • Keep it short. Keep it focused. Keep it relevant.

The course presentation ends with: “Thank you for your attention… any questions?”

Before and after

Added example: not from the course.

Before: twelve lines of small black text on one slide. After: a title, three key words in large letters and one graph with a single colour for the key result. And if the projector breaks, you can still present the three key words from memory.

Watch out

Relevant means useful and connected to the topic. Watch out: it is a false friend for French speakers.
