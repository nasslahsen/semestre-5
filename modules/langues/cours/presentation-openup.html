The exceptional presenter: OPEN UP

The course starts with a question: think of two exceptional presenters (a media personality, a politician, a professor, a business or religious leader). What do they have in common?

OPEN UP

The characteristics of an exceptional presenter are represented in the acronym OPEN UP: Organized, Passionate, Engaging, Natural, Understand your audience, Practice.

Why present?

The main objectives of a presentation: to inform, to entertain, to touch emotions, move to action. One presentation can aim at several of them.

Think about it

“Have you ever fallen asleep or felt bored during someone's speech, presentation or dialogue? If yes, give some reasons.” Typical reasons: the speaker reads, never looks up, speaks in the same tone, talks too long. Each one breaks a letter of OPEN UP.

An example

Added example: not from the course.

A blood donation campaign at the university: inform (how many bags are missing), touch emotions (a patient's story), move to action (“sign up today”).
