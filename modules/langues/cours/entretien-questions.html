Typical questions and bad impressions

Some questions come up in almost every interview. And some attitudes are enough to make a bad impression, even with a good CV.

Typical questions

FamilyQuestions from the course
About youTell me about yourself. Why did you choose that particular degree programme? What would you consider your major achievements to date?
About the jobWhat interests you about this job? What do you know about this organisation? What experience have you had that is relevant to this post? How do you see your career developing (5 years)?
General knowledgenews and your field (only the title is on the slide)

What creates a bad impression?

  • poor personal appearance
  • negative attitude
  • lack of interest and enthusiasm
  • lack of preparation
  • poor knowledge of the expected role
  • failure to give concrete examples of skills
  • over emphasis on money/rewards

Weak answer, strong answer

Added example: not from the course.

What interests you about this job? Weak: The salary is good. (money first). Strong: I'm interested in medical imaging, and this post would let me maintain MRI scanners, which I studied during my internship. It shows interest, knowledge of the role and a concrete example.
