II. True or false (6 points)

  1. Some American companies think that the productivity of Japanese workers is better than that of their workers.
  2. Piggly-Wiggly Carolina approached a large corporation with a buy-out offer.
  3. The employees were relieved because the owners of Piggly-Wiggly turned down the buy-out offer.
  4. Executives wore overalls at the party.
  5. Piggly-Wiggly employees borrowed a truck from the people who worked at Delta Airlines.
  6. The workers at Delta were happy because they had been given an 8 % raise even though Delta was losing money.
Voir la correction

Correction proposée :

  1. True : “Companies look at Japanese productivity with envy.”
  2. False : Piggly-Wiggly “was approached by a large corporation with a buy-out offer”.
  3. True : “The relieved employees organized a campaign…” after the owners “turned down the buy-out offer”.
  4. False : “truckers in overalls, executives in coat and tie”.
  5. False : they borrowed the idea : “The idea of giving something to their company had been borrowed from the people who worked at Delta Airlines.”
  6. True : “the quarterly report from Delta had shown a loss. But Delta had gone ahead and given an 8 percent raise.”

III. Answer the following questions (8 points)

  1. What are the two benefits the new American business would get if its workers feel committed to their jobs?
  2. Explain what is meant by “involvement teams”.
  3. In your country, what is the reaction of employees if their company is in trouble?
Voir la correction

Correction proposée :

1. If workers feel committed to their company, the business gets a stable work force, and its workers are as productive as they could be. As a result, it is more likely to succeed in the marketplace (paragraph 7).

2. Involvement teams are groups of employees at Control Data, similar to Japan's “quality circles”, which help workers to solve work-related problems by themselves (paragraph 6).

3. Question ouverte : pas de réponse unique. Exemple de réponse ajouté : In Morocco, reactions vary. Some employees accept extra work or delayed bonuses to save their jobs, especially in family companies where they know the owners. Others worry and start looking for a new job. As at Piggly-Wiggly, loyalty is stronger when managers listen to their staff and avoid layoffs. Ce qu'on attend : ton avis, deux ou trois idées développées, un exemple, un lien avec le texte.
