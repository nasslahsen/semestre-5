Introduction and voice

The first seconds decide whether the audience will listen. The course gives seven tips for the introduction and three for the voice.

An attractive introduction

  • Be in time
  • Smile and look at the audience
  • Start slowly
  • Use a genuine opening
  • Look organized
  • State the main objective(s) of your presentation
  • Dress nicely

Your voice

  1. Be heard
  2. Air intake
  3. Vary your voice (speed, pitch and volume)
WordMeaning
genuinesincere, real

pitchhow high or low your voice is
volumehow loud you speak
speedhow fast you speak

An introduction that follows the course

Added example: not from the course.

Good morning everyone. Last year, our hospital lost two days of scans because of one broken cable. Today, my objective is to show you a simple maintenance plan that prevents this. I'll cover three points. A genuine opening, a clear objective, a visible plan.
