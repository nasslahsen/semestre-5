B. Exercise 6 : synonyms and antonyms

A. Find a synonym : unprecedented, reliable, misleading, essential, acquire, modify, empathy, uncertainty. B. Find an antonym : flexible, reliable, innovative, complex, appropriate, independent.

Voir la correction

A. unprecedented → unparalleled / never-before-seen ; reliable → trustworthy / dependable ; misleading → deceptive / confusing ; essential → necessary / crucial / vital ; acquire → obtain / gain / learn ; modify → change / alter / adjust ; empathy → understanding / compassion ; uncertainty → doubt / unpredictability.

B. flexible → rigid / inflexible ; reliable → unreliable / untrustworthy ; innovative → traditional / conventional ; complex → simple ; appropriate → inappropriate / unsuitable ; independent → dependent.

Model answers of the answer key (open-ended questions : different answers are possible) :

  1. Critical thinking can be difficult to develop because it requires people to question their assumptions, evaluate evidence objectively, and consider different perspectives before reaching a conclusion.
  2. Artificial intelligence can generate ideas and assist humans in creative tasks, but human creativity involves imagination, personal experience, emotions, and the ability to understand complex social and cultural contexts.
  3. Schools should combine both. Knowledge is important, but students should also learn how to apply knowledge through problem-solving, critical thinking, communication, and practical activities.
  4. Because technology and professional requirements are constantly changing. Workers need to update their knowledge and acquire new skills throughout their careers.
  5. Students should develop communication, teamwork, critical thinking, creativity, digital literacy, adaptability, problem-solving, and emotional intelligence.
  6. Yes. Although some people may naturally have stronger interpersonal skills, emotional intelligence can be developed through self-reflection, communication practice, empathy exercises, and experience.
  7. Jobs involving highly repetitive and predictable tasks may be more susceptible to automation, such as some data-entry, routine administrative, and certain manufacturing tasks.
  8. New professions may emerge in areas such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, renewable energy, biotechnology, robotics, and digital education.
  9. They can develop transferable skills such as adaptability, creativity, critical thinking, communication, digital literacy, and lifelong learning rather than preparing only for one specific occupation.
  10. Technology could create job displacement, the need for continuous retraining, increased competition, privacy concerns, and greater pressure to adapt to rapidly changing working environments.
